# FRAMily Community

Welcome to the community hub of the International FRAM Community.

This repository hosts discussions, collaboration opportunities, community initiatives, and knowledge exchange related to the Functional Resonance Analysis Method (FRAM).

---

## Discussion Categories

### 📢 Announcements
Official news, releases, updates, and important information from the FRAMily community.

### 🤝 Collaborations
Connect with researchers, practitioners, educators, and students. Find collaborators, discuss project ideas, share opportunities, and build partnerships across domains and disciplines.

### 🌍 General Discussion
Open discussions about FRAM, resilience engineering, complexity, socio-technical systems, and topics relevant to the FRAMily community.

### 💡 Ideas & Future Directions
Share suggestions, proposals, visions, and emerging ideas that can help shape the future development of FRAM, the FRAMily community, and related initiatives.

### 🧩 Model Showcase
Share FRAM models, case studies, applications, and examples from research and practice. Showcase your work, receive feedback, and learn from others.

### 🔬 Research
Discuss publications, ongoing research, emerging developments, methodologies, and scientific questions related to FRAM, resilience engineering, and socio-technical systems.

### 💻 Software & Tools
Discuss FRAM-related software, tools, documentation, feature requests, technical issues, and development activities.

### 🎓 Teaching & Learning
Ask questions and share resources related to learning and teaching FRAM, including tutorials, exercises, courses, workshops, and educational materials.
